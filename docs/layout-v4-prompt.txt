Title: "fnc-fogger - Wiring Diagram".
Subtitle: "DRAFT 04 - ESP32-C3 SuperMini".
1. "24 V Supply": no power-supply illustration, only +24 V DC and GND input
terminals. In the original draft, +24 V served fan pin 2, fogger positive and
buck IN+. Common GND serves buck IN-/OUT-, ESP32 GND, fan pin 1 and MOSFET
source. Show the compact black XZG buck, 4R7 inductor and four functionally
labelled terminals, not assumed physical pad positions. Buck label: 24 V to
5 V. OUT+ to ESP32 5V, OUT- to GND. Note: "Check 5 V version / setting;
measure output before connecting." Do not claim LM2596, an adjustable trimmer
or a guaranteed 3 A rating.
2. "ESP32-C3 SuperMini": compact black board, USB-C, ceramic antenna.
Table: 5V from Buck OUT+ (5.0 V), GND common ground, 3V3 to sensors, GPIO0 SDA,
GPIO1 SCL, GPIO4 FAN_PWM, GPIO5 FAN_TACH input, GPIO6 MOSFET gate.
Note: "Proposed GPIOs - Check board pinout". No S3 board.
3. "Sensors - I2C": SCD41 CO2 0x62 and SHT45 humidity/temperature 0x44,
parallel 3V3, GND, SDA GPIO0, SCL GPIO1. Note: "Check I2C pull-ups to 3.3 V".
No chamber-interior label.
4. "Noctua NF-A4x10 24V PWM": pin 1 GND to common ground, pin 2 +24 V from
supply input, pin 3 TACH to GPIO5, pin 4 PWM from GPIO4. Notes: "Direct PWM -
3.3 V push-pull - 25 kHz", "Tach: internal ESP32 pull-up".
5. "Fogger - IRLB8721": front-view TO-220 pins 1 gate, 2 drain, 3 source;
metal tab is drain. GPIO6 to gate, fogger negative to drain, GND to source.
Original draft supplied fogger positive at 24 V; later corrected to 5 V.
No direct fogger-negative-to-GND connection, no added resistors. Original
notes: "Switching at 3.3 V not guaranteed. Reset state undefined without gate
pull-down."; "HIGH = ON, LOW = OFF; slow time windows"; "30 V maximum;
check voltage spikes". Later testing supersedes the switching warning.
6. "Control + OTA": SCD41 to CO2 PID to fan PWM; SHT45 to humidity PID to
fogger ON/OFF. Notes: OTA over WiFi, 5 V remains powered; initial USB install;
actuator firmware not yet implemented; updates/sensor faults request fogger
OFF; dry-run protection required. Later revisions use PI labels.
Footer: orange 24 V, red 5 V, blue 3.3 V, black GND, green I2C, purple GPIO.
"Common ground - Check board, load and switching before assembly".
"USB: disconnect external 5 V first". No mains, power-supply picture,
LM2596 or ESP32-S3. Prioritize exact functional tables over decorative wiring.
